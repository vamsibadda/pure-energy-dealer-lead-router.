What to Build Next for PURE Energy Dealers

If I joined PURE Energy, I would build an Interactive WhatsApp-Native Dealer Portal & Mobile App. 

Why This Priority?
Currently, dealers receive leads via the web dashboard, but field sales agents and showroom staff rely heavily on WhatsApp for rapid customer communication. Bridging this gap will directly increase conversion rates for 3kWh, 5kWh, and 10kWh home power solutions.

Key Capabilities
1. Instant WhatsApp Lead Alerts: Push new lead notifications directly to assigned dealers with one-click "Click-to-Call" or "Click-to-Chat" actions.
2. Offline Inventory & Quote Generator: Enable dealers to calculate system requirements based on home load and generate branded PDF quotes on mobile.
3. Automated Follow-up Reminders: Trigger automated WhatsApp updates when lead statuses transition from Contacted to Converted.

By bringing lead routing and quote management into WhatsApp, we eliminate portal login friction, drastically shorten response times, and drive measurable revenue growth across the dealer ecosystem.
